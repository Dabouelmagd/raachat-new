FEATURES

What the app actually does today

Everything listed here is built and tested. What is still being prepared is stated plainly at the end of this section.

💬

Full messaging

Text, voice notes and attachments; edit and delete; quoted replies, reactions, forwarding and starred messages. Three delivery states: sent · delivered · read.

📞

Voice and video calls

Direct device-to-device calls, tested between two phones on two different networks. Call audio is never recorded on our side.

👥

Groups and channels

Groups have no member limit. Roles and admins, public or private, with group discovery. Channels are broadcast: the owner posts, subscribers read and react.

🎙️

Audio rooms

Live rooms for up to 50 people at once, with a temporary identity that exists only inside the room and disappears when you leave.

⏱️

Stories and polls

24-hour stories grouped per account, and polls inside any conversation — the author decides whether voters are shown by name or only counted.

Pharaonic stickers

25 stickers drawn for this app: Anubis, the Sphinx, the Eye of Horus, Cleopatra, Nefertiti, the pyramids, a felucca, a fanous and more.

🤝

Find people you know

We match your contacts against registered users. The numbers are never stored on our side and never written to any log — matched in the moment, then gone.

🔤

Quick translation

Instant translation of everyday phrases between Arabic, English and French, working offline from a dictionary inside the app.

🎨

Make it yours

Text size, bubble color, chat background, and a light or dark mode — and the layout grows with the text instead of clipping it on small screens.

Still being prepared — stated exactly as it stands

  • SOONWallet and bill payments: the screen exists and top-up is closed until we connect a licensed payment partner. The app itself says "soon", so nobody walks through the flow and gets turned away at the last step.
  • SOONSign-up by mobile number: registration is by email for now; SMS needs a contract with an Egyptian aggregator.

  • SOONFull sentence translation: the phrase dictionary works; machine translation is not switched on yet.
GETTING STARTED

You are in within a minute

No install, no card, no phone number — an email and a password.

Open it in your browser

On a phone or a computer: app.raachateg.com. On a phone, choose "Add to Home Screen" from the browser menu and it behaves like any installed app.

Create an account with your email

Your name, email, a password and your date of birth; a confirmation code arrives by email. The app is for adults aged 18 and over.

Start a chat or join a group

Allow contact matching to find people you already know, open "Discover groups" to join a public one, or create your own group and share its link.

One image per placement

The placements have different proportions, so a single image would be cropped. You upload an image per placement, and the admin panel warns you before approval if yours will be cut.

Impressions and clicks

Every impression and click is recorded per placement, so you know which ones earn their keep before you renew.

No behavioral targeting

Ads are first-party, and there is no third-party tracking SDK anywhere in the app. Every user sees the same catalog.

Images are hosted by us

Creative is uploaded to us; external image URLs are rejected — so that review means something, and so no user's address is handed to a third party.

Prices include VAT

The agreed price is the total: VAT is extracted from it, never added on top — no surprise line at the bottom of the invoice.

Set durations and gift codes

One, three, six or twelve months, booked in whole months so the invoice is a clean number. Gift codes are available for promotions.
